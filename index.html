Questions, answered

Is ZeeRun a replacement for my doctor or physio?

No — and it will tell you so. ZeeRun handles the everyday training decisions (run, modify, stop) and screens for red flags. When something looks serious, it tells you to see a clinician instead of an app.

Do I need an Apple Watch or fitness tracker?

No. The morning readiness score works best with sleep and heart-rate data from Apple Health, but everything else — niggle logging, verdicts, plans, ladders — works with just your phone.

How is this different from Strava or Nike Run Club?

Those apps are brilliant at recording what you did. ZeeRun is built around what you should do next — it factors in pain, recovery, and injury risk before planning your session, not after.

What happens to my health data?

It stays on your device. ZeeRun reads Apple Health data to compute your readiness and verdicts, but doesn't upload your health data to its servers.

I'm currently injured. Should I still download it?

Yes — that's exactly when the return-to-run ladders earn their keep. Log where you are, follow the rungs, and let the plan respect your recovery instead of rushing it.

Is there really a free tier?

Really. GPS tracking, daily verdicts, and niggle logging are free forever. Pro ($9.99/mo or $59.99/yr) adds readiness scores, ladders, and deeper plan adaptation.
